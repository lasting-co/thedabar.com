The idea

Scripture, without the noise.

DABAR is built around a simple conviction: God's Word is worth slowing down for.

Instead of surrounding Scripture with endless distraction, DABAR gives you a focused place to read, listen, explore, and study. Start with the text. Go as deep as you want.

What it does

Five ways in.

Read

The King James Version in a simple, printed-Bible-inspired layout built to keep your attention on the text. Highlight, bookmark, take notes, save prayers.

Listen

Scripture read aloud, following your reading position verse by verse. Listen while you read, or close your eyes and hear the Word.

Explore

Tap a Greek or Hebrew word and go to the source.

  • Original language and transliteration
  • Meaning and grammar
  • Strong's number
  • Pronunciation

Connect

Cross references and related passages without losing your place. See the connections. Follow the thread. Let Scripture illuminate Scripture.

Understand

Scripture, cross references, word studies, commentary and other study sources in one focused experience.

The Study Guide

An optional AI assistant you can ask about a passage. It points you back to Scripture and its sources.

The Guide is a study assistant — not Scripture, and not a final authority on doctrine. It is off until you turn it on.
